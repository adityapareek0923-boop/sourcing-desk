Refund and return policy

When we refund, when we replace, and how to open a claim.

You get a full refund if

  • We fail to ship within the lead time on the quote, you give us 7 days to recover, and we still cannot ship.
  • The parts arrive and fail incoming inspection against the specification on the quote.
  • The parts are found to be counterfeit under our Counterfeit policy.
  • You cancel an order before we have placed the supplier purchase order on your behalf.

Partial refund or replacement

If part of a shipment is affected (wrong lot code, visual damage, missing from pack), we will replace the affected quantity or refund it, whichever is practical, within 10 business days of your claim.

Non-refundable

  • Orders already shipped and inspected-passed where you later change your mind (regulated-parts stock is non-returnable to our suppliers).
  • Damage caused after delivery.
  • Delays caused by customs or carrier beyond our control, except as stated above.

How to claim

Email hello@chipsharksupply.com within 10 business days of delivery with: your RFQ ID, photos of the parts and packaging, a description of the fault, and your preferred remedy. We acknowledge within 2 business days and resolve within 10 business days.
