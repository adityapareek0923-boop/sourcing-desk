Privacy policy

What we collect, why we collect it, and how we handle it.

What we collect

When you submit an RFQ (Request for Quote), we collect: your name, business email, company, phone number (optional), the manufacturer part numbers and quantities you want to source, lead-time target, and any notes you add. If you pay, our payment processor collects billing details on our behalf — we never see your card number.

Why we collect it

To source the parts you asked about, send you a quote, process payment if you accept, and ship your order with the required paperwork. We also use your email to send order-status updates and reply to your questions.

Who we share it with

  • Supabase (database + email webhook; EU/US).
  • Resend (sends your confirmation and quote emails on our behalf).
  • Stripe (payment processing; your payment details go directly to Stripe).
  • Supplier network: we share only the part number, quantity, and lead-time target when sourcing. We do not share your name, email, phone, or company with suppliers unless you ask us to.

We do not sell your data. We do not use advertising trackers.

How long we keep it

Active RFQ records: as long as the account is open plus 7 years for tax/audit records. You can ask us to delete your record at any time by writing to hello@chipsharksupply.com, except information we must keep by law (e.g. invoices).

Your rights

You can ask us to show you what we have on you, correct it, or delete it. Email hello@chipsharksupply.com. We respond within 30 days.

Security

Data in transit is encrypted (HTTPS). Database access is restricted and secrets are stored in Supabase Vault. We log admin actions. We will notify affected buyers in writing if a breach exposes their personal data.
