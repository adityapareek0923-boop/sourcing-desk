Counterfeit-parts policy

How we vet stock, what we document, and what we do if a counterfeit slips through.

Three-tier vetting

  1. Supplier screening. We buy only from stock partners that pass our vetting (business registration, trade references, prior lot history, published returns policy).
  2. Document check. Every lot ships with a Certificate of Conformance from the supplier and, where available, upstream traceability to the manufacturer or franchised distributor.
  3. Physical inspection. We apply the inspection level stated on your quote: visual + mark-permanency on all orders by default; electrical, decap, or X-ray on request. Inspection photos are included with the shipment.

What counts as counterfeit here

Parts whose manufacturer, part number, lot code, or specification does not match what the manufacturer actually produced; parts remarked, reconditioned, or sold as new when they are not; parts failing basic authenticity tests (acetone/mark permanency, X-ray of lead frame, decap of die).

If a part turns out to be counterfeit

  • Full refund within 10 business days of our confirmation.
  • We cover return shipping (or you destroy the parts and we accept photo evidence).
  • We blacklist the supplier that provided the lot and refund you from our own operating funds, not the supplier.
  • We file a report with ERAI (Electronic Resellers Association International) within 15 business days.

How to claim

Email hello@chipsharksupply.com with your RFQ ID, inspection evidence (test report, photos, decap results), and the sequence of events. We acknowledge within 2 business days.
