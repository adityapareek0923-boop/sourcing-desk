How we protect your BOM

Your BOM is your product. Here is exactly what happens to it when you send it to us.

Suppliers see one line at a time

When we source, a supplier sees one part number and the quantity. They never see your name, your company, your product, the rest of your BOM or where it ships, until we place an order with them. Shipments are blind drop-shipped: no supplier invoice or pricing in the box.

NDA on request

Ask and we sign your NDA, or send you our mutual NDA, before you share the BOM.

Private storage

  • Your files are kept in storage only ChipShark can reach.
  • Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest.
  • Changes to your request are logged.
  • One customer's data is never shared with another customer.

Deletion

Ask and we delete your BOM and files. Order records and invoices are kept as long as tax law requires; see our privacy policy.

What we decline

We don't accept ITAR-controlled items, Controlled Unclassified Information (CUI) or anything that requires CMMC. If a request includes any of these, we decline it and delete the files. We also decline orders for sanctioned destinations or parties.

Questions

Write to hello@chipsharksupply.com. We answer security questionnaires for procurement and supplier-onboarding reviews.
