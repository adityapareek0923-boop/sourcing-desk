Questions

Why do I pay before you buy?

Shortage stock sells within days and prices change weekly. Paying on approval lets us lock the lot at the quoted price. If the part fails your incoming inspection within 30 days, you get a full refund including return shipping.

How do you avoid fakes in a shortage?

Shortages pull counterfeits into the market. We disclose the supplier tier, get lot and date-code photos before you pay, serial-check GPUs, and can route a sample through a third-party lab at your cost.

What if the price moves after the quote?

Quotes are held for 48 hours. After that we re-check stock and price before you pay, and tell you if either changed.

Do you ship to India and the Middle East?

Yes. Sources span North America, Europe and Asia, and we pick the closest reliable one to your drop-off to keep transit and duty down.
